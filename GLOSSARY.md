# Slopstagram

Slopstagram collects Instagram stories and preserves observations of them in reports.

## Language

**Story**:
An Instagram story identified independently of the reports in which it appears.

**Sticker**:
An annotation attached to a story, including a location, mention, music, hashtag,
or link.

**Sticker occurrence**:
One instance of a sticker on a story. Separate occurrences may have the same kind
and label.

**Canonical story data**:
The shared information about a story, independent of any individual report.

**Report snapshot**:
The information captured about a story for a particular report. It retains that
report's historical view separately from canonical story data.
